What this measures

How faint a striped patch can you still see?

You will see circular patches of faint bars that lean left, stand upright or lean right. Swipe in the direction they lean. Patches get fainter and finer; one eye at a time; about three minutes per eye.

Step 1 of 3

Hold the phone where you would read a message

The front camera estimates how far the screen is from your eyes so the patches can be drawn at the right size. Nothing is recorded or sent; only the distance number is kept. About 40 cm is ideal.

Camera not available, or want to check it?

One-time check (optional, improves accuracy): measure 40 cm from your eyes to the screen with a tape or ruler, hold there, and tap.

Step 2 of 3

Cover your left eye

Use your palm, without pressing on the eye. Keep wearing your usual glasses or contacts. The right eye is tested first.

  • Swipe up-left if the bars lean left, straight up if upright, up-right if they lean right.
  • Guess if unsure. Tap Can't see only when there is no pattern at all.
  • The first two patches are easy and count as practice if you get them wrong.
